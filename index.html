About FundSprout

FundSprout is a local-first student finance companion. Your data is stored in this browser's local storage by default. If Cloud Sync is configured, your data can also be synchronized with your Google Sheet — there are still no accounts or sign-ins.

On overspending: FundSprout allows a purchase to bring your balance below zero, but will always ask you to confirm first. This mirrors real-world spending and keeps the app flexible if you log an expense before its matching allowance.

Version: 1.1 · Built with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap 5.3, vanilla JavaScript, and Chart.js. No backend, no build tools, no tracking.
